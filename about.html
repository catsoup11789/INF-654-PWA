Meet Marvin

“I could contemplate infinity. Instead, we’re choosing lunch.”

A chronically bored supercomputer for everyday choice paralysis. Not life advice. Just a small nudge with a very large sigh.

Less magic. More attitude.

Food, spending, and work or study keywords choose a matching answer pool. Other questions get something cryptic. Too short or unclear? Expect confusion. Even genius has limits.

Operating instructions

  1. 01

    Submit your tiny crisis.

    Type a question or click Dictate to turn speech into text. Pizza, purchases, procrastination: all welcome.

  2. 02

    Disturb the supercomputer.

    Click Consult the void to reveal a decision. On mobile, shake to mix the answers instead. Less wrist, same wisdom.

  3. 03

    Receive. Sigh. Carry on.

    Read the verdict, or click Read aloud to hear it. Find past questions and decisions in your Log.

The core can outlast the signal.

When Offline readiness says “Saved for offline use,” the core experience, including local answer pools, works without a connection. Open each other page online once before expecting it to be available offline. No signal required for a sigh.

Dictate depends on browser speech-recognition support and microphone permission; offline recognition also needs a local engine. Read aloud needs browser speech support and a local voice to work offline. If either is unavailable, type your question or use Consult the void.

Give the void a home
